Project Overview

Nilea is a luxury Egyptian juice brand, inspired by the historical fruit fields of the Nile delta. The brand centers around ancient Egyptian history, organic freshness, and royal heritage.

The goal of this commercial was to formulate a highly luxurious, macro cinematic production showcasing the package design, intricate gold-engraved details, and dynamic fruit collisions.

Client Brief

The campaign guidelines highlighted fresh splashes, macro droplets, premium textures, and traditional heritage iconography. The presentation needed to look extremely prestigious and clean, avoiding generic commercial setups.

We designed slow-motion liquid simulations reacting to glass surfaces, mapping gold hieroglyphs onto 3D bottle geometry, and using macro lighting to highlight fresh juice swirls and product facets.
